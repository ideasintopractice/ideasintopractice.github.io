Use an organization-approved AI tool. Do not upload confidential client or employee information. Keep the original CSV unchanged, verify calculations and source evidence, and label FACT / ASSUMPTION / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN. An AI-generated recommendation is not an approval.

QUESTIONS

Good to know

Do I need a paid AI subscription?

No particular paid service is required by these datasets. Use a tool approved by your organization that supports CSV or pasted-text analysis. Features and upload limits vary.

Are the datasets real client data?

No. All records, organizations, scenarios and financial amounts are fictional and intended for training.

Can I complete the book without downloading files?

The book's concepts and printed instructions stand alone. The downloadable files enable the complete hands-on dataset exercises.

Where are the facilitator answer keys?

Facilitator verification keys are not published here, so learners can attempt exercises independently. Organizations conducting instructor-led sessions can use their separately supplied facilitator pack.

What if a link stops working?

The printed book should use the final verified website URL. Save a local copy of the datasets for continued offline use.
